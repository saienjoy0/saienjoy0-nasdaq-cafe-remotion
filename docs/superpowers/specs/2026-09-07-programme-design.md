# Programme audit remediation

NASDAQ Cafe explains how events in US equities connect to businesses, policy and supply chains in East Asia when evidence makes that connection material. Viewers should leave with a changed understanding of a causal question.

## Design decisions

Keep editorial judgment upstream and the renderer deterministic. Preserve immutable historical bundles and their schema. Correct subtitle token fragmentation without changing narration or audio. Causal graphics must reflect explicit supported edges, never adjacency in a narrative list. Company-specific and market-wide explanations must remain separate unless an evidenced transmission connects them.

Introduce story-first planning: hook, question, development, discovery and meaning are required functions, not nine mandatory containers. Variable scene production requires a coordinated version migration across Plot schemas, freeze/acceptance, renderer, TTS blocks and diversity windows. Do not falsely enable it by weakening array checks alone.

The programme review must identify actual scene/object evidence for findings and distinguish unreviewed visual/audio output from passed structural validation. East Asia assessment covers structural business exposure independently of chronological price transmission. Generated images are a Visual Intelligence capability, selected against real media/data/existing images; never manufacture precise graphs or documentary evidence.

## Acceptance

Regression examples: Applied Materials and 102.5 must not fragment across subtitle lines/pages when tokens fit. Preserve normalized wording and cue time coverage. Graphs must not imply the AMAT-specific explanation causes oil/retail macro events. New editorial requirements must be mechanically checkable in the current authoring/review path where feasible. Legacy production stays reproducible; explicitly document any migration not yet enabled. No automatic final render, merge or publication.
